DEMO FLOW:
1. Open index.html and optionally Register as Evacuee.
2. Login as Administrator.
3. Review a pending registration on Dashboard and click Approve & Issue QR.
4. Open Accommodation Map and assign the approved evacuee/group to an available hallway or Main Lobby.
5. Login as Evacuee / User to see the QR and accommodation state.
6. Admin can scan the QR on QR / Barcode Scanner to simulate check-in.
